# Second-hand clubs store scaffold

Entry points: **More → Second-hand clubs** and the Club page. Placeholder route: `/second-hand-clubs`.

The future store should support searching by brand, model and club type, and adding listings with club details, condition, photographs and pricing/value.

Every club value must be labelled **pro shop credit**, including listing cards, search results and the eventual add/edit flow. It is credit to spend in the pro shop, not a cash payment. The placeholder shows `€— pro shop credit` to establish this convention without inventing a listing or valuation.

Current scope is deliberately limited to the app links and a signed-in, themed preview page with disabled search/add controls. There are no listings, database migrations, APIs, transactions, payments or credit balances. Listing workflow, who approves values, availability and credit redemption remain future design decisions.
