WhatsApp message 1 - Activate your account

Hi all. To activate your Interclub Snooker account:

1. Open the invite email from Interclub Snooker.
2. Tap Activate your account.
3. Set your password, or use Google if it is the same email address you were invited on.
4. After you are in, bookmark https://www.interclubsnooker.com on your phone.

Only invited users can activate an account.

WhatsApp message 2 - Managers: pick your team

Managers: to pick your team for a match:

1. Open Interclub Snooker and go to Fixtures.
2. Tap your team’s match for the week.
3. Pick the players into the match positions.
4. Save the team first.
5. Only press Publish when the team is final, because publishing locks it.

When both teams publish, the match opens for scoring.
Attach: 02-pick-team-whatsapp.png

---

WhatsApp message 3 - Managers: scores and breaks

Managers: when a match is being played:

1. Open the active match from Fixtures.
2. Select the winner of each game.
3. Record breaks only where there is a break worth recording.
4. When every game is entered and checked, press Match Completed.

Once completed, the match is locked and the league table updates.
